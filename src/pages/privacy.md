This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use server-side tracking.

**Google Analytics**: I use Google Analytics (GA4) to measure page views. GA4 records country-level location, device/browser type, and pages visited. IP anonymization is enabled (`anonymize_ip: true`).

**Cookies**: Analytics cookies are only set after you click "Accept" on the consent banner shown on first visit. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is stored locally in your browser.

**No other tracking**: I do not collect email addresses, serve ads, or use any other third-party trackers.
